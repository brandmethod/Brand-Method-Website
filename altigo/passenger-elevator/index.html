THE RANGE

Two configurations

FOUR DRIVE PLATFORMS

Altigo passenger lifts come in a machine room (MR) configuration for mid- and high-rise buildings, and a machine-room-less (MRL) family for tighter shafts and shorter rises. A compact ≤450 kg variant suits homes and low-traffic floors. All run 2:1 traction reeving with centre-opening doors.

MR · TRACTION 2:1

Machine Room

Permanent magnet gearless traction across the full load range, with speeds up to 2.5 m/s for taller buildings.

Load630–2000 kg
Speed1.0–2.5 m/s
Car height2400 mm

MRL · TRACTION 2:1

Machine Room Less

No separate machine room — the drive sits inside the shaft. Defined overhead (4350–4950) and pit (1500/1600) per speed.

Load630–1600 kg
Speed1.0 / 1.75 m/s
Pit1500 / 1600 mm

COMPACT · ≤450 KG

Compact / Home

A small-footprint variant for residences and low-traffic floors, with a shallow pit and reduced overhead.

Load≤ 450 kg
Pit320 mm
Overhead4150 mm

Smaller shaft

Optimised structure leaves more car within a tight shaft.

2400 cabin

Integrated ceiling car height across the range.

Powerloss levelling

Emergency battery brings the car to the nearest floor and opens.

IoT monitored

Cloud service watches operation 24/7 for fast maintenance.

Medical elevator

Altigo medical lifts are purpose-designed for hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centres and nursing homes. Safety and hygiene are the primary considerations — making every move one of care and warmth.

Safe, comfortable, stable

Large space for easy access

Priority emergency call

Anti-interference design
