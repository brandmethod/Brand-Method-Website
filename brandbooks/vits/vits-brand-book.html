Company Introduction

One of Malaysia’s pioneer noodle manufacturers. Fifty years of making the everyday bowl, and exporting it.

Vit’s began in 1975 as a passion for making noodles. From a small kitchen in Selayang it has grown into a leading Malaysian halal-certified noodle brand, exporting to more than thirty countries.

The name comes from the Mandarin Wei Yi — the only one. The phoenix that carries it stands for power, renewal and longevity. Vit’s intends to be Malaysia’s one and only noodle expert of choice.

01 · Brand1.2 · Positioning and Script

Positioning and Script

One statement everyone agrees on, and the thirty seconds you say out loud when someone asks what Vit’s is.
Positioning statement
For every household that wants a good bowl without a second thought, Vit’s is Malaysia’s noodle expert — halal since 1980, made in Rawang to global food safety standard, and trusted in more than thirty countries.
The thirty second script

“Vit’s has been making noodles in Malaysia since 1975. We started in a small kitchen in Selayang. Today we run a 105,000 square foot plant in Rawang and ship to more than thirty countries.

We took halal certification in 1980, before anyone asked us to, because a bowl of noodles should be something every Malaysian can share. Everything we make is built to FSSC 22000, HACCP and GMP standard.

We make our own brands, and we make other people’s — OEM and private label, from air-dried to fresh noodle. If it is a noodle, we know how to make it well.”

Who it is for

Malaysian households first. Then distributors, retailers and OEM partners who need a manufacturer that will not embarrass them.

What we make

Instant, fresh and air-dried noodles under Vit’s, and under partner brands through OEM and private label.

Why it matters

Halal, safe and consistent is not a claim here. It is audited — FSSC 22000, HACCP, GMP and SMETA.

Vision and Mission

The vision is old and it has not moved. The mission is how it gets kept.
Vision
Shared prosperity through something simple — a bowl of noodles welcome in every Malaysian home, regardless of race or religion.
Mission

Make it halal, always

Certification since 1980 is not a market tactic. It is the condition of being on every Malaysian table, whatever the faith of the household.

02

Hold the standard

03

Carry Malaysia outward

Export the authentic taste of Malaysia — Penang white curry, asam laksa, Hokkien mee — to Asia, the Middle East, Europe and beyond.

04

Make it for others too

OEM and private label built to the same standard as our own brands, for partners worldwide.

Three brand faces and one fallback. Archivo carries the personality; Figtree carries the reading.

Headlines, the wordmark, pack front-of-face and any type over 30px. Set expanded at 112–118% width; it is what gives the brand its shelf voice.
Figtree

Body copy, decks, pack back-of-face, long-form. Warm humanist proportions that stay legible at 6pt on film.
JetBrains Mono
