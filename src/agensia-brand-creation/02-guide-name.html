Why a funnel and not a brainstorm

A brainstorm produces names the room likes. A funnel produces names that survive contact with a register, a search engine and a competitor. The gates are ordered so the cheapest disqualification happens first.

Where most projects fail

At gate 5. A name that has cleared every creative test and no legal test has cost the project a month. Legal screening moves earlier the more crowded the category is.

Gate 7 is a decision, not a discovery

The funnel does not choose. It removes everything that cannot be chosen, and hands the client a set where every option is defensible. The choice that remains is a strategic one.

Agensia entered this document at gate 7. The name is confirmed and registered in Brand Book V3.0. The pages that follow record why it holds, not whether it should.

Name & Category Sample
Six name types, what each one buys you and what each one costs

Descriptive

Says what the company does, in the words a buyer would use.

WorkflowEngine · ComplyAI · AutoFlowBuys instant comprehension. Costs ownership: hard to register, impossible to defend, and dates the moment the product widens.

Suggestive

Points at the benefit without naming the mechanism.

Salesforce · Slack · StripeThe best commercial balance. Understood on second contact, still ownable. Most enterprise brands should sit here.

Coined

Built from a root and a suffix. Means nothing before you fill it.

Agensia · Celonis · VerizonHighest ownership, widest legal room. Costs launch effort: the meaning has to be supplied by the brand, not the word.

Compound

Two real words fused into one new one.

Salesforce · Facebook · PayPalReads instantly and registers well. Risk of sounding assembled rather than designed if the halves fight each other.

Founder or heritage

A person, a place or a date.

Siemens · Rolls Royce · BoschCarries credibility and continuity. Costs flexibility: the story is fixed, and a founder exit becomes a brand problem.

Acronym

Initials standing in for a longer descriptive name.

IBM · SAP · ABBOnly works after decades of spend, or as a shortening the market invents. Never a starting position.
Reading this page

Ownership rises left to right. Comprehension falls in the same direction. The choice is always how much launch effort you are willing to spend to own the word outright · and in a regulated category, where the buyer reads before they feel, a coined name has to be paired with a descriptive line that carries the category.

The Power of Brand Names
Seven tests. A name that fails two of them will be replaced within five years.
TestWhat it asksPasses whenFails when
SayableCan a stranger say it correctly on first sight, and spell it back after hearing it once?One pronunciation only, and the spelling follows the soundTwo plausible readings, or a silent letter
PortableDoes it survive every market the company plans to enter, in every language spoken there?No unintended meaning, no impossible consonant clusterCarries a meaning nobody intended in a target market
OwnableCan it be registered, held and enforced in the relevant classes?Clear in class, clear in register, handle availableDescriptive terms that no register will grant exclusively
ExtendableDoes a second product break it?Sub-names attach without strain or explanationThe name describes the first product only
DurableDoes it outlive the technology it launched on?Names a relationship, a role or an ideaNames a mechanism, a model generation or a year
DistinctCould a buyer confuse it with a competitor at a glance?Different rhythm, different opening soundShares a prefix with three others in the category
DrawableDoes it make a good mark?Balanced letterforms, a usable initial, a workable lengthFifteen letters, or an initial with no shape in it
The two that get skipped

Durable and Drawable. Both are invisible at the moment of choosing and both are expensive later. A name that dates to a technology cycle has to be replaced; a name that will not draw forces the identity to carry weight the word should have carried.

Two failures is the threshold

One failure is survivable and usually manageable with a descriptive lockup. Two compound: a name that is neither ownable nor distinct has no defence and no recall, and no amount of design will supply either.
