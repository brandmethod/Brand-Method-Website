Combination rule

One curve, many passes. Elements 1 to 4 are not four ingredients to be mixed, they are four properties of a single construction. Nothing is added to the mark: depth is produced by repetition, never by a gradient, a shadow or a second element.

Why rows 6 to 10 are here

So the client can see what was considered and why it was set aside. An element table is the answer to "why this shape" in a room the designer is not in, and rows 6 to 10 are half of that answer.

The aperture  ·  four treatments of the counter at the centre
TreatmentElement table matchResult
Open counter, even spacingAperture + offset repetitionCarried. The counter stays open at every size, which is what keeps a dense mark legible and gives the A its shape
Filled centreAperture closedReads as a target or a button. The mark gains weight and loses the human space the whole concept rests on
Continued to the centreOffset repetition, no apertureVisually rich at 1024 px and solid black by 32 px. The failure mode a contour mark has to be designed against
Aperture pulled off-centreAperture + threefold symmetryAdds direction and movement, and breaks the threefold balance. Held as a possible motion state, not as the static mark

Drawn on a concentric circle to isolate the variable. In the finished mark the same treatments apply to a three-lobe form, and the differences are the same ones.
The aperture is the only element of the four that can be lost by accident: it closes on its own as the mark shrinks unless the contour count is reduced with it.
This is why 24 px is the specified minimum for the symbol, and why a solid alternate exists below it.

RouteElement table matchWhy it was set aside

Shown in grey because none of these is a live option. They are here so that a reader who asks "did you consider the obvious one" has an answer with a drawing attached.
The first three are competent marks. That is the point: a competent mark that belongs to the category rather than to the brand is the most common way an identity fails.
The fourth is the only route on this page ruled out before a pencil touched it, and it is ruled out by the messaging pillars rather than by taste.
