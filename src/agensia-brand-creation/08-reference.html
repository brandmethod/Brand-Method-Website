How to read this page

Competitor names are set typographically here rather than reproduced as artwork. This page fixes the comparison set and what we are reading it for, not the artwork itself. Nothing below is a claim about any individual company or mark.

Colour territory

Blue is the enterprise-software default and orange is the one loud exception. Green is genuinely unoccupied at platform level in this category, which is unusual and worth more than most differentiators a brand can buy.

Blue dominant  ·  orange and purple as the exceptions  ·  black for platform brands  ·  green open

Form language

The category splits two ways: the incumbents draw literal machinery, connected nodes, arrows and loops; the newer tools draw a bright geometric glyph that could belong to any software company.

A contour mark that resolves into a letter belongs to neither group, and that separation is the opportunity. It is also the harder thing to execute, because line density is unforgiving at small sizes.

What to read for

Judge each mark on four things only:

1  Does it survive at 24 px in a procurement deck?
2  Does it read enterprise without reading generic?
3  Would a compliance officer take it seriously?
4  Could it be mistaken for one of the others?

Question 4 matters most. In a category this crowded with blue geometry, confusion is the default outcome rather than the risk.
