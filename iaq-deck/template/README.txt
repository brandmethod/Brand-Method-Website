IAQ-Company-Deck-2026.pptx
    The presentation, all 67 pages of it, exactly as it stands. Nothing
    has been taken out and nothing has been restyled.

    Behind them, pages 68 to 88 are the master template: an index and
    twenty empty layouts. They are HIDDEN, so they never appear when
    you present or export to PDF, but they sit in the slide panel where
    you can see them.

    TO ADD A NEW SLIDE
      1. Scroll to the end and find the layout that fits.
      2. Right-click it > Duplicate Slide.
      3. Drag the copy to where it belongs in the deck.
      4. Right-click it > Hide Slide, to un-hide it. (A copy of a
         hidden page is hidden too, so this one click matters.)
      5. Type over the guide text.

    THE SAME LAYOUTS, THE POWERPOINT WAY
    They are also wired into the slide master, so in PowerPoint you can
    use Home > New Slide and pick one by name from the gallery: it
    reads IAQ, Title; IAQ, Comparison; IAQ, Org chart and so on. In
    Google Slides the same list is under Slide > Apply layout.
    PowerPoint's own eleven starter layouts have been taken out of that
    menu, since picking one of those is how a deck goes off-brand.

THE TWENTY LAYOUTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

01  Title                       Open a deck or a chapter of one
02  Section divider             Open a new part of the deck
03  Statement                   One argument, said plainly, with a photo
04  Highlights                  Three or four figures that carry the page
05  Icon cards                  A set of capabilities or principles
06  Comparison                  Two or three options read side by side
07  Org chart, reporting tree   Who reports to whom, three levels
08  Org chart, flat team band   A team of peers, no hierarchy implied
09  Org chart, group structure  A holding company and its businesses
10  Process                     Stages that run in order, start to finish
11  Timeline                    Dates in order, with what happened at each
12  Infographic                 One visual in the middle, the parts around it
13  Image and specs             One project, pictured and specified
14  Programme                   Work packages against a calendar
15  Table                       Rows and columns, where detail has to be exact
16  Full-bleed image            Let one photograph carry the page
17  Closing                     End the deck: the ask, and how to reach you
18  Agenda                      What the deck covers, before it covers it
19  Quote                       Let a client, or the board, say it instead
20  Before and after            Two states of the same thing, side by side

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKING WITH IT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Photographs are real pictures: right-click one and choose Change
Picture to swap it. The frame and the crop stay put.

Need a row, a column, a card or a step more than the layout gives you?
Copy one that is there and paste it. It keeps the type and the spacing.

Do not restyle inside a page. If a layout is not working for what you
are saying, you are probably on the wrong layout. Pick another one.
That is what the twenty are for.
