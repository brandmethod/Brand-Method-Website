What is already fixed

Mark, palette, typeface and ratios are confirmed in Brand Book V3.0 and are not in question here. The stylescape is choosing a temperature within a settled system, not proposing an alternative to it.

The ratio every tier has to hold
46 PAPER + MIST 34 INK 12 8

Twelve per cent is the whole green budget for a surface, and the mark is already spending part of it. A tier that reads as green-forward has broken the ratio before anyone has looked at the photography · which is the specific failure the Open Studio tier exists to make visible.

Hard constraints, carried from the brand book
Green is a signal

The mark, one call to action per surface, nothing else. It never carries text on a light ground and never indicates a status.

No gradients in the mark

Depth comes from line density alone. A gradient may appear in a photograph. It may not appear in the identity.

Ardela Edge is a display face

Titles, statements, section numerals and the wordmark. Never a paragraph, in any tier, at any temperature.

Mist is the default ground

Not white and not ink. The off-white with a trace of green in it is the surface most of the brand actually sits on.

Photography brief  ·  client to supply

Real operations, shot on client sites wherever permission allows. Aerospace assembly, clean rooms, production floors, packing lines. Natural light. No blue tech grade, no lens flare, no glowing overlays, no holographic interfaces, and no stock people pointing at a screen. Machinery is photographed as it is, including wear. People are photographed working, not posing. Frame loose enough to crop to both 16:9 and 1:1.

Why this matters more than usual here

A regulated buyer can tell the difference between a real plant and a rendered one in under a second, and the whole brand argument is that Agensia understands their operation. Stock imagery would undo more than it saves.

What the outer tiers are still for

They are boundaries, not alternatives. Machine Room is the point past which the system stops inviting anyone in; Open Studio is the point at which green stops being a signal. A campaign that drifts past either edge is out of system.
