High-performance control system

Energy saving & regeneration

By sensing passenger flow the escalator adjusts its speed curve to save energy across the whole travel. VLR technology monitors the drive's DC busbar voltage and feeds regenerated energy back to the grid.

VLR regenerationFlow-adaptive speed

Planning Guide

LOGICAL POSITIONING, BY TRAFFIC

Arrangement follows the building — traffic volume, flow direction and footprint. Five layouts cover most cases, from a single run linking two levels to crisscross banks that move crowds through several floors at once.

One-way

Single unit

Links two levels. Best where traffic runs mainly one way; reversible by time of day.

One-way

Continuous

Stacked runs, same direction, no detour. Links three sales levels; needs more space.

One-way

Interrupted

Offset runs force a short walk between levels, leading shoppers past displays.

Two-way

Parallel

Up and down side by side. For heavy two-way flow; economical with three or more units.

Two-way

Crisscross

Compact X-banks for large stores and transit; minimises travel time between levels.
