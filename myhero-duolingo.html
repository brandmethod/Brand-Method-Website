Reading the tree

Depth is deliberate. Anything at level three is a module, not a brand — it never gets its own mark, its own colour or its own name in the app store. Only MyHero is marketed.

The one hard rule

The Record Engine never surfaces. It has no logo, no name in the interface and no mention in marketing. When a parent sees its output, the credit goes to the clinic — "checked by your clinic" — never to the system.

Growth path

If MyHero later serves clinics beyond Elite Care, the parent node changes and everything below it survives intact. This is the main reason the name avoids any clinic-specific reference.

Colour territory

Blue is the category default — it signals clinical safety, which is exactly why almost everyone reaches for it. MyHero cannot differentiate on hue. Sky stays, because the client chose it and it is correct; the separation has to come from form, warmth and typography.

Blue dominant · teal and blush secondary · black for platform brands

Form language

The category splits two ways: government and telehealth marks are geometric and cool; femtech and paediatric marks are rounded and soft, often to the point of reading as a toy.

The kickstart put MyHero between them — credible enough for a clinic, warm enough for a parent. That gap is the opportunity, and it is genuinely under-occupied.

What to read for

When the artwork lands, judge each mark on four things only:

1  Does it survive at 24px?
2  Does it read medical without reading governmental?
3  Would a tired parent trust it?
4  Could it be mistaken for one of the others?

Question 4 is the one that matters most — a crowded blue category makes confusion the default outcome.

Decision

MID — Playful Guardian. It takes what makes Duolingo work — the shape language, the pressable button, the path, the character — and refuses what would make MyHero a copy: the green, the leagues, the hearts.

This direction reopens a kickstart decision

Kickstart ruled out the cape and flagged the human figure as mascot risk. A Duolingo direction cannot exist without a character — it answers that risk with an animal, not a person. If the client will not carry a mascot at all, drop this direction rather than dilute it.

Why not simply use the green

Feather green is Duolingo's asset, and in a clinical context green already means result normal. Taking it would cost MyHero both ownership and a piece of its clinical vocabulary.

Red is not a game colour

Duolingo spends red on lost hearts and wrong answers. Here red is reserved for clinical urgency — an abnormal result, an overdue vaccination. Nothing about points may ever be red.

Contrast floor

Sky on white is 3.1:1 — strong enough for a large chunky button, not for body text. Clinical copy is always Ink; sky is a surface colour, not a reading colour.

What locked must mean

Done mint · now flame · upcoming sky · locked grey. Locked describes the future only — a date that has not arrived. It may never mean "you have not earned this", and every past node stays tappable for as long as the record exists.

Guardrails
The six rules that let a health record be playful without becoming a game — these are not style notes, they are conditions of the direction
Never gate the record

No mechanic may ever stand between a parent and their own child's medical history. No hearts, no locks on data, no "come back tomorrow". Locked nodes describe the future, never the archive.

Never shame

A missed appointment is a fact, not a failure. No sad face, no broken streak graphic, no "you let Hero down". The passive-aggressive-owl register is explicitly out of bounds — it is funny for a language app and cruel for a parent whose child was ill.

Never rank families

No leagues, no leaderboards, no percentile against other parents. A child's health is not a competitive field, and a parent losing that comparison is being told something about their child that no product should say.

Never monetise a health action

No currency, no purchasable relief, no reward that costs money to keep. The moment a parent can pay to protect a streak about their child's vaccinations, the brand has crossed a line it cannot come back from.

Streaks belong to logging

A streak may count days the record was kept current. It may never count doses taken, symptoms logged for a chronic condition, or appointments attended — those are clinical outcomes, and attaching a game to them changes what a parent reports.

Clinical surfaces stay plain

Results, dosages, allergies, certificates and everything the clinic sees are set in plain type on white with no character, no colour coding and no progress language. The game lives on the path; the record lives on paper.

The test to apply

Before any mechanic ships, ask one question: would this still feel right to a parent whose child is seriously ill? A path, a badge and a warm reminder pass. A lost streak, a league place and a countdown do not. If a feature only works for the healthy majority, it does not belong in a medical record.
