THE SPECIALIST STANDARD
01 The story · 1994 to today

Founded in 1994 by Mr. Tiew Soon Aik in Malaysia, IAQ began as a cleanroom specialist and became a Total Facility Solutions Provider: engineering design and consultation, procurement, construction, testing and commissioning, and lifetime maintenance, delivered as one accountable partner.

Today the group serves semiconductor, data centre, EV and life-science owners across seven countries, has delivered to the highest cleanroom class, and is preparing for listing. The brand carries that record: the specialist proven to deliver the environments that cannot fail.
