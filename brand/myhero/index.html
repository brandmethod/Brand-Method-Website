Name Funnel Reference

How a name survives

A name is not chosen, it is eliminated down to. Every stage removes candidates on a single test, so the name that reaches design has already cleared meaning, memory, law and availability.

Rule of thumb

Start with 100+ candidates. Expect 50–60% to fall out at trademark and domain screening alone. Anything that reaches stage 7 must be defensible, pronounceable and free.

Working backwards

A founder-led name usually enters at stage 7 rather than stage 1. When that happens the funnel is run in reverse — the candidate is tested against every earlier stage before it is accepted, not after.

1 Establish criteriaCategory, tone, audience, must-avoid list
2 Generate 100+ candidatesDescriptive, suggestive, abstract, coined, founder
3 Meaning & tone filterCut anything that mis-signals the category
4 Pronunciation & recall testSay it, spell it, hear it back a day later
5 Trademark & domain screen50–60% typically fall out here
6 Legal & linguistic checkClass search, negative meaning in market languages
7 Shortlist 2–3 into designOnly names that can carry a mark proceed
8 Select & registerFinal name locked, filings begin

The name positions the brand not as a product, but as a role the parent already plays.

In one sentence, it makes room for

— A record that is theirs, not the clinic's
— A tone that reassures instead of instructing
— A future beyond one clinic

What the name deliberately avoids

Any reference to artificial intelligence, any clinical Latin, any word that would date the product to a technology cycle, and any construction that sounds like a government service.

Decision status

Aligned at kickstart — the team reached consensus on the descriptive name My Health Record, with MyHero as the spoken and searched brand.

Reading the tree

Depth is deliberate. Anything at level three is a module, not a brand — it never gets its own mark, its own colour or its own name in the app store. Only MyHero is marketed.

The one hard rule

The Record Engine never surfaces. It has no logo, no name in the interface and no mention in marketing. When a parent sees its output, the credit goes to the clinic — "checked by your clinic" — never to the system.

Growth path

If MyHero later serves clinics beyond Elite Care, the parent node changes and everything below it survives intact. This is the main reason the name avoids any clinic-specific reference.

Content & Design Reference
The register to write in, and the register to design in
Content reference — how it should read

"Your next scan is on Wednesday 3 September, 10:30am."

Weekday and month spelled out. No codes, no room numbers up front.

"Everything in this test looked normal."

The answer first. Raw values are available underneath, never above.

"Dr Amira would like to go through one of these with you at your next visit."

Names the person. Never alarms a parent a clinician has not yet spoken to.

"Nothing here yet. Your clinic will add to this after your next visit."

Empty states explain what happens next, rather than reporting zero results.

"AI-generated clinical summary · 0 results returned · ⚠ ABNORMAL VALUE FLAGGED"

All three break a rule: names the technology, reports like a database, alarms without a clinician.
Design reference — how it should look
Air over density

If a screen feels busy the fix is more neutral, not less colour.

Cards, not tables

Dense tabular layouts are for the clinic-side view only.

Combination rule

One container plus one cue. Elements 1, 5, 6 and 7 are containers; elements 2, 3 and 4 are cues. A mark takes exactly one from each group — two cues inside one container reads as clip art.

The idea

Care arrives in fragments — a visit here, a result there. The pause between them is where parents lose the thread. MyHero is the mark in the middle that holds both halves together.

How it could enter the mark

Two vertical strokes — the pause, the gap, the two halves of a record — could sit inside the shield in place of the cross, giving the mark a second reading that is genuinely ownable.

Status

Concept only. Presented so the founder's framing is on record before route selection. Not drawn as a finished mark and not one of the three carried routes.
