The Brief

Great spaces are felt.
The brand had to feel the same.

With two decades of experience and a team of over 30 designers, KLAAS already had the portfolio. What it needed was a brand presence to match: content that shows the finish, detail and process behind every project, and a home online that turns interest into consultations.
